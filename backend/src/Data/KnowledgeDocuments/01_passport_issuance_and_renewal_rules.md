# DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA
## DEPARTMENT OF IMMIGRATION AND EMIGRATION
### Extraordinary Gazette & Administrative Circular No. DIE/OPS/2026/01
**Title:** Regulations Governing the Issuance, Renewal, and Endorsement of All-Countries Sri Lankan Passports

---

### 1. Statutory Authority
These regulations are promulgated under the Immigrants and Emigrants Act No. 20 of 1948 (as amended) and govern all Ordinary Passport (All-Countries / "N" Series) applications submitted via the Government Service Navigator digital intake or regional immigration counters.

---

### 2. Mandatory Eligibility Criteria
1. **Citizenship:** Applicant must be a verified citizen of Sri Lanka by descent or registration. dual citizens must present dual citizenship certificates.
2. **Age Threshold:**
   * Adults: 16 years of age and above are eligible for a 10-year validity passport.
   * Minors: Under 16 years receive a 3-year validity travel document with mandatory parental consent affidavits.
3. **Identity Verification:** The applicant must possess a National Identity Card (NIC) issued by the Department of Registration of Persons (DRP). The 10-digit legacy format (ending with 'V' or 'X') or the 12-digit format is strictly verified.

---

### 3. Required Evidentiary Documents
Every applicant must provide high-resolution digital scans and produce originals at the counter:
1. **Original Birth Certificate:**
   * Must be a certified extract issued by the Registrar General's Department within the preceding six (6) months.
   * "Probable Age" certificates or damaged/unclear documents require Grama Niladhari corroboration.
   * If born outside Sri Lanka, the Consular Birth Certificate and Citizenship Certificate issued under Section 5(2) of the Citizenship Act.
2. **National Identity Card (NIC):**
   * Clear copy of both front and reverse sides. Name and date of birth must match the birth certificate.
3. **Previous Passport (for Renewals):**
   * Original previous travel document. The bio-data page, observation pages, and any existing valid visas will be inspected.
   * In case of a lost passport, a certified Police Report and Form D (Lost Passport Declaration) along with a penalty fee of LKR 15,000 are mandatory.
4. **ICAO Biometric Studio Photo Acknowledgement:**
   * 3.5cm x 4.5cm color photograph taken at an authorized digital studio meeting ICAO biometric standards.
   * Studios upload biometrics directly to the Department's central server and provide the applicant with a 15-digit Photo Studio Acknowledgement Barcode Slip.

---

### 4. Statutory Fee Schedule (Effective 2026)
In accordance with Ministry of Public Security Gazette regulations, the following statutory levies apply:
* **Normal Service (Delivery within 14–21 working days):** LKR 10,000.00
* **One-Day Urgent Processing (Same-day dispatch/collection):** LKR 20,000.00
* **Penalty for Altered or Damaged Passport:** LKR 20,000.00
* **Payment Modalities:** Integrated online card payment, electronic government bank transfer, or People's Bank / Bank of Ceylon counter deposit voucher.

---

### 5. Biometric Counter Protocols & Fulfillment
1. **Mandatory In-Person Counter Appearance:**
   * Following digital document review approval (Milestone 1), all applicants must appear in person for digital fingerprint enrollment (ten-finger capacitive capture), facial scan confirmation, and electronic digital signature recording.
2. **Designated Processing Centers:**
   * Head Office: "Suhurupaya", Subhuthipura Road, Battaramulla.
   * Regional Branches: Kandy, Matara, Vavuniya, and Kurunegala Regional Immigration Offices.
3. **Collection:** Passport may be collected at the counter upon presentation of the Verification Pass with QR code, or dispatched via Sri Lanka Post Registered Insured Courier to the residential address.
