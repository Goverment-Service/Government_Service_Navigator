# DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA
## DEPARTMENT OF MOTOR TRAFFIC (DMT)
### Operational Circular No. DMT/LIC/2026/04
**Title:** Standard Operating Procedures for Renewal and Extension of Motor Vehicle Driving Licenses

---

### 1. Legal Basis & Jurisdiction
Under the Motor Traffic Act (Chapter 203 of the Legislative Enactments of Sri Lanka, as amended) and Motor Traffic (Driving Licences) Regulations, all motor vehicle driving licenses issued in Sri Lanka are subject to periodic physical, mental, and aptitude verification before renewal.

---

### 2. Mandatory Eligibility & Medical Prerequisites
1. **Age Threshold:** Minimum 18 years for Light Vehicles (Classes A, A1, B, B1) and minimum 21 years for Heavy Commercial / Passenger Vehicles (Classes C, C1, CE, D, D1).
2. **National Medical Certificate (NTMI Clearance):**
   * Applicants must undergo a comprehensive medical examination at the National Transport Medical Institute (NTMI).
   * Medical certificate must be valid (issued within the last six (6) months).
   * Examinations test visual acuity, colour discrimination, auditory response, blood glucose levels, cardiovascular stability, and mental fitness.
   * Commercial and heavy vehicle drivers must undergo mandatory ECG and psychological stress evaluation.

---

### 3. Required Evidentiary Proofs
1. **National Medical Fitness Certificate (NTMI):** Original certificate signed by a registered NTMI Medical Officer with official stamp and barcode.
2. **Current Driving License:** Original existing driving license card or laminated booklet. If expired beyond five (5) years, a practical competency re-assessment is required.
3. **National Identity Card (NIC):** Clear scan and original for counter verification.
4. **Permanent Address Verification:** If the applicant's residential address has changed from the original license record, an original utility bill or Grama Niladhari certificate issued within 3 months is required.

---

### 4. Statutory Fee Structure (DMT Tariff 2026)
* **Light Vehicle License Renewal (Classes A, A1, B, B1):** LKR 2,500.00
* **Heavy Vehicle License Renewal (Classes C, C1, CE):** LKR 3,300.00
* **Commercial Passenger Vehicle License Renewal (Buses / Coaches - D, D1):** LKR 3,500.00
* **Late Renewal Surcharge:** LKR 250.00 for each calendar year of delay past the expiry date.
* **Conversion to Smart Card Format (Optional Replacement):** LKR 1,200.00

---

### 5. In-Person Counter Appearance & Biometrics
1. **Physical Counter Attendance:** Following digital verification of the NTMI medical certificate and license records, the applicant must attend a designated DMT branch.
2. **Counter Biometric Capture:**
   * High-definition digital portrait photograph captured against standard backdrop.
   * Electronic signature recorded via DMT digital signature pad.
   * Physical surrender of previous license for cancellation and electronic chip programming.
3. **Authorized DMT Centers:**
   * National Center: Department of Motor Traffic Driver Licensing Complex, Werahera, Boralesgamuwa.
   * District Secretariats: DMT District Offices in Kandy, Kurunegala, Anuradhapura, Hambantota, Gampaha, and Kalutara.
