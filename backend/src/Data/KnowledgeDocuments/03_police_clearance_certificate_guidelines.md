# DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA
## SRI LANKA POLICE HEADQUARTERS
### Administrative Directive No. PHQ/CL/2026/02
**Title:** Protocols and Guidelines for the Issuance of Police Clearance Reports for Foreign Employment, Visas, and Migration

---

### 1. Overview & Objective
The Police Clearance Branch of the Sri Lanka Police Headquarters issues background check certificates verifying that an applicant has no recorded criminal convictions, pending indictments, or warrants within the jurisdiction of the courts of Sri Lanka.

---

### 2. Dual-Stage Multi-Departmental Workflow
In accordance with national security directives, Police Clearance Reports follow a mandatory two-tier verification procedure:
* **Stage 1: Local Grassroots Verification (Grama Niladhari & Local Police Station):**
  The applicant's residential history is verified locally by the Grama Niladhari of the relevant division and corroborated by the Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of the territorial Police Station having jurisdiction over the residential address.
* **Stage 2: Central Criminal Records Registry Check (Police HQ Clearance Branch):**
  The Central Criminal Record Bureau (CCRB) and Intelligence Division perform forensic fingerprint cross-referencing and name-index matching against nationwide court registries.

---

### 3. Required Documentation
1. **Clear Copy of National Identity Card (NIC):** High-resolution scan showing full 10-digit or 12-digit number, photograph, and signature.
2. **Valid Passport Copy:** Bio-data page and pages containing endorsements and existing travel history.
3. **Grama Niladhari Certificate of Residence:**
   * Must explicitly state the applicant has resided in the mentioned division for the stated period (minimum continuous residence verification).
   * Must be duly countersigned and stamped by the Divisional Secretary of the respective administrative division.
   * Certificate validity is strictly three (3) months from date of issuance.
4. **Employer or Embassy Requirement Letter (if applicable):** Document stating the requirement for a Police Clearance Certificate (e.g. Australian Home Affairs, Canadian IRCC, UK Home Office, or licensed Foreign Employment Agency).

---

### 4. Statutory Fee & Processing Timelines
* **Standard Clearance Search & Certification Fee:** LKR 1,500.00
* **Dispatch & Postal Tracking Levy (Registered Speed Post):** LKR 300.00 (within Sri Lanka) / LKR 3,500.00 (Diplomatic Pouch / Direct Embassy Dispatch).
* **Processing Duration:** 14 working days from the date of final OIC local field report receipt.
* **Certificate Validity:** Police Clearance Certificates are valid for six (6) months from the date of issuance.

---

### 5. Collection and Diplomatic Routing
Applicants may specify whether the final sealed certificate should be:
1. Collected at the Police Headquarters Clearance Branch Counter, Colombo 01;
2. Dispatched via insured registered mail to their local address; or
3. Transmitted directly to the Consular Affairs Division of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Ceylinco Building, Colombo 01) for official apostille and diplomatic authentication.
