# DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA
## MINISTRY OF PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION & PROVINCIAL COUNCILS
### DIVISIONAL SECRETARIAT ADMINISTRATIVE REGULATION
**Title:** Registration of Business Names, Individual Proprietorships, and Partnerships

---

### 1. Statutory Provisions
Registration of sole proprietorships and unincorporated business partnerships is governed under the Business Names Statute of the respective Provincial Council (e.g. Western Province Business Names Statute No. 04 of 2011) and the Business Names Ordinance No. 16 of 1918. Any individual commencing trade under a business name other than their true surname must register within fourteen (14) days of commencement.

---

### 2. Multi-Agency Workflow Stages
1. **Stage 1: Business Name Search & Clearance:**
   The applicant proposes up to three (3) distinct business trade names. The Divisional Secretariat verifies against the Central Registry of Business Names to prevent trademark infringements, misleading state terminology (e.g. "Ceylon", "National", "Imperial"), or identical registered names.
2. **Stage 2: Physical Premises Inspection & GN Report:**
   The Grama Niladhari of the Grama Niladhari Division where the enterprise operates conducts an in-person physical inspection of the premises, verifies owner's deed or valid lease agreement, and ensures no residential zoning violations exist.
3. **Stage 3: Tax Identification Linkage (Inland Revenue Department):**
   Under statutory gazette regulations, all businesses must possess a valid Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) issued by the Inland Revenue Department prior to certification.
4. **Stage 4: Divisional Secretary Approval & Certificate Release:**
   Final review by the Assistant Divisional Secretary or Registrar of Business Names and release of the Form B (Certificate of Registration).

---

### 3. Required Evidentiary Attachments
1. **Form A (Prescribed Application for Individual / Firm):** Duly completed statement of particulars signed by the proprietor.
2. **Business Name Search Approval Slip:** Clearance certificate issued by the Business Names Registrar.
3. **Grama Niladhari Premises Inspection Report:** Form DS/GN/BN-01 signed by the GN and stating clear boundary demarcation and ownership/tenancy status.
4. **Proof of Premises Right:** Deed of transfer, certified rent/lease agreement on stamped paper, or written consent of the property owner with their NIC copy.
5. **Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) Certificate:** Issued by Inland Revenue Department.
6. **National Identity Card (NIC):** Proprietor's original and photocopy.

---

### 4. Statutory Fees & Stamp Duty
* **Statutory Registration Fee:** LKR 1,500.00
* **Provincial Council Stamp Duty:** LKR 1,000.00
* **Total Payable to Divisional Secretariat:** LKR 2,500.00
* **Late Registration Penalty:** Surcharge of LKR 500.00 if submitted after 14 days of trade commencement.
