# DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA
## REGISTRAR GENERAL'S DEPARTMENT
### Circular No. RGD/BD/2026/03
**Title:** Rules and Procedures for the Search of Civil Registries and Issuance of Certified Extracts of Death Registrations

---

### 1. Legislative Authority
Under the Births and Deaths Registration Act (Chapter 110 of the Legislative Enactments of Sri Lanka, as amended), certified copies of entries in any register kept under this Act are admissible in court as prima facie evidence of the facts stated therein.

---

### 2. Entitlement and Legal Kinship Restrictions
To safeguard privacy and prevent fraudulent property transfers or estate tampering, applications for Death Certificate certified extracts are strictly restricted to:
1. **Next of Kin (Immediate Family):** Surviving spouse, legitimate children, parents, or legal siblings.
2. **Authorized Legal Representatives:** An Attorney-at-Law or proctor acting under a registered Power of Attorney or representing the estate in testamentary proceedings.
3. **Government / Judicial Entities:** Officers requiring certified extracts for state pension, widow/orphan pension (W&OP), insurance claim, or judicial inquiry.

---

### 3. Progressive 3-Stage Workflow
1. **Stage 1: Entitlement & Kinship Verification:**
   The verification officer verifies the applicant's kinship through applicant's birth certificate or marriage certificate.
2. **Stage 2: Central / District Registry Record Search:**
   Upon approval of entitlement, the registry search officer searches the physical folios and electronic indices of the relevant Registration Division and year.
3. **Stage 3: Certified Copy Printing, Stamping, and Issuance:**
   The certified copy is stamped with government revenue stamps, sealed by the Additional District Registrar, and handed over or dispatched.

---

### 4. Required Documentation
1. **Applicant National Identity Card (NIC):** Full clear copy of the applicant.
2. **Proof of Lineal Kinship:**
   * For spouse: Marriage Certificate.
   * For son/daughter: Applicant's Birth Certificate showing deceased as parent.
   * For attorney: Power of Attorney or Bar Association credentials with client authorization.
3. **Declaration of Cause of Death / Burial Record (if available):**
   * Hospital Form 41 (Certificate of Cause of Death issued by Government Medical Officer).
   * Report of the Inquirer into Sudden Deaths (for inquest cases).
   * Burial or Cremation Permit issued by the Municipal Council / Urban Council.
4. **Registration Details:** Date of death, place of death (hospital, town, village), and Registration Division name.

---

### 5. Statutory Fees & Stamp Duty
* **Registry Search Fee (for 2 years or less):** LKR 200.00
* **Extended Search Fee (each additional year beyond 2 years):** LKR 100.00 per year
* **Certified Extract Stamp Duty & Issuance Fee:** LKR 500.00 per copy
* **Standard Cost for 1 Copy:** LKR 700.00
* **Standard Cost for 2 Copies:** LKR 1,200.00
* **Payment Channels:** Online card payment, GovPay mobile payment, or counter payment at any Divisional Secretariat Cashier.
